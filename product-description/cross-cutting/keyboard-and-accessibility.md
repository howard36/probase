# Keyboard and accessibility

## Summary

This document gathers what Probase offers someone who does not use a mouse, who uses a screen reader, or who needs to see where keyboard focus is: every key that does something, what Tab reaches on each page and what it cannot reach, where focus goes when the page changes, and what assistive technology is told about each page and control. Probase has no keyboard shortcuts of its own. The keys that matter are [Enter](../glossary.md#input), [Shift/Ctrl/Cmd+Enter](../glossary.md#input), [Escape](../glossary.md#input), Space and Tab inside particular controls, and each control's own document owns what they do there; this document lists them side by side and says what they add up to.

Most of the site works from the keyboard: every link, button, checkbox, switch and open text box can be reached with Tab. Three kinds of control cannot, because they are clickable boxes rather than buttons: the [heart](../glossary.md#interface), a [click-to-edit](../glossary.md#interface) field while it shows its text, and the two cards on the [chooser](../glossary.md#the-site). For screen readers, every page is called "Probase", following a link announces nothing, and many fields have no name.

## The simple case

A ViewOnly member who uses only the keyboard opens a collection page. Nothing has focus. Tab reaches the search box, the four subject checkboxes and the "Archived" switch, then each [card](../glossary.md#interface) in turn; Enter on a card opens the problem. On the problem page, Tab goes from the back link to the subject chip, passes over the title and the heart, and stops on "Show spoilers"; Enter shows the answer and solution. More presses of Tab reach the comment box, "Post comment", then "Previous" and "Next". This member can read, filter, open spoilers, comment and testsolve from the keyboard. They cannot like a problem.

An author doing the same finds that their problem's title, statement and answer cannot be opened for editing from the keyboard. A member of a collection that [requires testsolving](../glossary.md#testsolving) who has not chosen a testsolver type cannot get past the chooser at all.

A screen-reader user hears "Probase" as the name of every page and hears nothing when a link loads a new one. Error [toasts](../glossary.md#interface) are read out as they appear. The countdown in a timed attempt, a wrong answer, and the change from the locked to the unlocked view are not.

## Focus

### On arrival

No page puts focus anywhere on arrival, with one exception: on the add-problem page every click-to-edit field starts open and takes focus as it appears, which leaves the cursor in "SOLUTION" without scrolling to it. After a full load, the first Tab goes to the first control on the page.

### When the page changes

Probase does not manage focus. When the control that has focus disappears, nothing takes its place: focus falls back to the page itself, and in Chromium the next Tab continues from where the vanished control was. This happens:

- After following any link inside the app, including a card, a chip, "Previous", "Next" and a page number: the new page arrives with nothing focused.
- When a click-to-edit field closes, whether saved (Enter, Shift/Ctrl/Cmd+Enter, "Save changes") or abandoned (Escape, "Discard").
- When the Add Solution box closes with Escape or "Discard", or is replaced by the solution after a successful submit.
- When "Start testsolving" succeeds: the button is replaced by the statement and the answer box, which does not take focus. Likewise when a correct answer, "Give Up" or the countdown ends the attempt.
- When a toast disappears after its 8 seconds while its "Dismiss" has focus.

Focus moves by itself in three places: into the Add Solution box when "Add Solution" is pressed, into an empty answer's box when "Show spoilers" is pressed by a user who can edit the problem ([spoilers](../problem-page/spoilers.md)), and back into a click-to-edit editor when its save fails. It stays where it is when a checkbox, switch or the search box changes the list, when a comment is posted (the box empties), when a wrong answer empties the answer box, and when a successful action [refreshes](../glossary.md#interaction) the page around it.

An empty one is visible only by its placeholder, if it has one, and by the focus ring while it has focus. The empty answer's box on the problem page has no placeholder, so it shows as the "ANSWER" caption with nothing under it.

## Screen readers

### Page names and announcements

Every page's title is "Probase": the collection page, every problem, the add-problem form, the test page, the error pages. Browser tabs, history and bookmarks cannot tell pages apart, and a screen reader announces only "Probase" when a page loads in full. The page language is English.

Following a link inside Probase announces nothing at all. The framework announces an in-app page change by reading the new page's title, but only when it differs from the previous one, and it never does.

> Technical note: Next.js's route announcer compares `document.title` before and after each client-side navigation and speaks only a change. The title comes from the single static `metadata` in `app/layout.tsx`; no page sets its own.

### Icons, color and math

Every icon (the heart, the lightbulbs, the padlock, the arrows on "Previous" and "Next", ✕ and ✓ on the leaderboard, a toast's exclamation mark and ×) is hidden from screen readers. As a result:

- The difficulty is not conveyed at all; the lightbulbs are the only place it appears.
- Whether the user has liked a problem is shown only by the heart's color.
- On the [leaderboard](../testsolving/leaderboard.md), a row reads as a rank, a name and bare numbers; the table has no header row, and the user's own row is marked only by its yellow background.
- On the chooser, the chosen card is marked only by its violet border.
- On cards, a problem's subject is shown by the color of its ID; the ID's letter carries it too.
- The back link's arrow, the search box's magnifier and the Google logo are drawings with no text of their own, which screen readers generally skip.

[Math](../glossary.md#interface) is readable: each formula carries a MathML copy for screen readers while the drawn version is hidden from them, and a formula that cannot be drawn is read as its source text ([math rendering](math-rendering.md)).

## Cancel and interrupt

What each interruption does to keyboard focus and to what a screen reader hears. "Before editing" means focus rests on a control with nothing typed; "while editing" means focus is in a text box or an open editor holding typed text. What happens to the text itself belongs to each control's document.

| Event                               | Before editing                                                                                                                | While editing                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Escape or Discard                   | No effect. Outside text boxes Escape does nothing, and it never dismisses a toast.                                            | Abandons a click-to-edit edit or the Add Solution box; focus is then lost rather than returned to the field or a button. In the search box, clears the search (Chrome, Safari). Elsewhere nothing. |
| Browser back or forward             | The previous page is shown with nothing focused, and nothing is announced.                                                    | As before editing. The typed text is lost as the control's own document describes.                                                                                                                 |
| Reload                              | A full load: a screen reader announces "Probase", and the first Tab goes to the first control.                                | As before editing; the typed text is lost.                                                                                                                                                         |
| Tab or window closed                | No effect.                                                                                                                    | No effect beyond losing the typed text.                                                                                                                                                            |
| A link inside the app followed      | The new page arrives with nothing focused and nothing announced.                                                              | Clicking the link first takes focus from the box, which saves a single-line or autosaving editor ([click-to-edit](../foundations/click-to-edit.md)); then as before editing.                       |
| Network lost mid-request            | No effect until something is sent.                                                                                            | The error toast is announced as an alert; focus stays where it was. A failed click-to-edit save reopens the editor with focus in it.                                                               |
| Request fails or returns an error   | No effect.                                                                                                                    | As network lost: the toast is announced, focus stays, and a failed editor reopens with focus.                                                                                                      |
| Session ends                        | No effect on the open page.                                                                                                   | "Not signed in" is announced as a toast; focus stays.                                                                                                                                              |
| Access changes                      | No effect on the open page.                                                                                                   | The permission toast is announced; focus stays.                                                                                                                                                    |
| Same record changed in another tab  | Not shown until a refresh, and a refresh does not move focus or announce anything.                                            | As before editing.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Same record changed by another user | As another tab.                                                                                                               | As another tab.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Autofill writes into the field      | No effect.                                                                                                                    | The browser's suggestions for a single-line box or an answer box open with the arrow keys; picking one is the same as typing it.                                                                   |
| The window loses focus              | No effect; focus is on the same control when the window comes back.                                                           | A single-line or autosaving editor saves and closes, so focus does not come back to it. Any other box keeps focus and its text.                                                                    |
| The testsolve time limit passes     | The testsolving view refreshes into the unlocked view with nothing announced. Focus on the answer box or its buttons is lost. | As before editing; the typed answer disappears with the box.                                                                                                                                       |

After an interruption, focus is wherever it fell, which after most of them is nowhere in particular; apart from a toast, nothing tells a screen-reader user what changed.

## Edge cases

- Links drawn as buttons ("Add Problem", "Back to home", "Continue to {collection}") do not respond to Space, only to Enter.
- Past the end of the problem page's Tab order, focus goes to the browser's own controls and then back to the back link; the title and the heart are skipped on every lap.
- An author who presses "Show spoilers" on a problem with an empty answer finds focus moved into the answer box. Tabbing away leaves it open and unsaved, since an empty box is never saved.
- Escape in the Add Solution box discards everything typed, however long, with no confirmation and no way to get it back.
- A toast can disappear while the user is still tabbing towards its "Dismiss", which is always last in the Tab order.
- A keyboard user filling in the add-problem form with Tab cannot go back to a field they have left: it closed as they left it, and Shift+Tab skips it.
- Several problems open in separate tabs all read "Probase", so neither sighted users nor screen-reader users can tell the tabs apart by name.
- The like count is part of every card's link text, read between the title and the statement (after the statement on a window under 640 pixels) with nothing saying what it counts.
- There is no dark mode. Dark colors are defined but never switched on, and the site ignores the system's dark setting.

## Open questions and verification

- Confirmed in a local pass (headless Chromium, production build): the Tab order on the collection page and on the problem page as listed, with the heart and the title never taking focus; Tab on the chooser reaching nothing; Enter doing nothing on a subject checkbox while Space ticks it, and Enter flipping "Archived" with focus kept; no visible focus indicator on the search box and the comment box, and rings on the checkboxes, switches and "Post comment"; focus moving into the Add Solution box and into an empty answer's box, and falling to the page after Escape in the Add Solution box (the next Tab went to "Add Solution"); nothing focused and the route announcer empty after opening a card and after Back; "Probase" as the title and no level-1 heading on the collection, problem and test pages; `lang="en"`; the Add Solution box drawn with no border in the page's own color.
- The chooser cannot be completed from the keyboard. This looks like a bug, and a serious one: in a collection that requires testsolving and does not make new members Serious, a keyboard-only member, Admins included, cannot open the collection at all.
- The heart and click-to-edit fields having no keyboard access look like bugs.
- No in-app navigation is ever announced, because every page has the same title. Giving each page its own title would fix both the silent navigation and the indistinguishable tabs. This may be worth treating as a bug.
- The unnamed fields (the search box, the answer boxes, the add-problem fields and menus, the problem page's editors), the missing focus indicator on the search box and the comment box, and the borderless text boxes look like bugs.
- Whether screen readers actually announce toasts was not tried with a screen reader. The region holding toasts is created only when the first toast appears, and some screen readers ignore a live region that arrives already filled; each toast is also an alert, which screen readers generally announce when it is inserted.
- The countdown and the wrong-answer line not being announced was read from code (neither is a live region); not tried with a screen reader.
- Where the next Tab goes after focus is lost varies by browser; only Chromium was tried.
- A screen reader's own click (Enter in the browse mode of NVDA or JAWS, a double tap in VoiceOver) sends a click to whatever is under its cursor, so it may operate the heart, the chooser's cards and click-to-edit fields that a keyboard alone cannot. Not tried.
- Escape clearing the search box is the browser's behavior for search fields; not tried, and Firefox may differ.
- Whether the Enter that picks an autofill suggestion also reaches the field (saving it, or submitting a form) was not tried.
- Color contrast was not measured.
